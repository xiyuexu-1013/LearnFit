      A NOTE FROM THE MAKER

      Hi. This is a project called LearnFit.

      I made it because study advice is often weirdly loud: work for this many minutes, rest for that many, repeat until you feel guilty. I wanted something quieter.

      LearnFit starts with your baseline and helps you notice changes as you work, so you can experiment, adjust, and find a study rhythm that fits your life. It is an experiment—not a diagnosis, a verdict, or a measure of your worth.

      — Xiyue, building in public

      FROM A FIXED TIMER TO A PERSONAL RHYTHM

      Build log

      MAR–JUN 2026 · THE QUESTION

      Why should one timer fit everyone?

      Pomodoro timers helped me, but my own limit changed by task and by day. I began asking whether a tool could help learners discover a rhythm instead of prescribing one.

      JUL 05 · FIRST PROTOTYPE

      I turned eye landmarks into a live signal.

      The Python version connected MediaPipe, EAR, blink detection, a score, WebSocket, and a dashboard. It worked—and immediately exposed camera, threshold, and missing-data problems.

      JUL 12 · DEBUGGING

      A blink needed a beginning and an end.

      I rebuilt blink counting as a state machine, separated quick blinks from long closures, added a 30-second baseline, and stopped trusting one noisy frame.

      AUG 07 · BROWSER REBUILD

      I removed the Python setup barrier.

      I moved MediaPipe and the scoring engine into browser JavaScript, separated the core modules, and made the first Chrome extension while keeping camera processing on-device.

      SEP 18 · HONESTER SCORE

      The score became a clue, not a verdict.

      I kept the 50/30/20 structure but compared it with each learner’s baseline, showed tracking gaps honestly, removed pupil-millimeter claims, and redesigned the interface.

      SEP 19–20 · REAL USE

      I built for life outside the demo tab.

      An offscreen Chrome extension can continue a user-started session across ordinary tabs. I also added consent-based testing, a research dashboard, and the public Pages deployment.

      SEP 25 · TASK-AWARE FOCUS

      I stopped treating every open tab as study.

      The extension now scores only pages the learner verifies, separates off-task and no-signal time, and withholds a final focus estimate when tracking coverage is below 60%.

       WHAT LEARNFIT CAN ACTUALLY TELL YOU

      A focus estimate with clear boundaries.

      LearnFit compares blink frequency, completed blink duration, and eye openness with your own usable 30-second baseline. The components are 50 points for blink frequency, 30 for blink duration, and 20 for eye openness; the result is smoothed over time.

      The website estimates focus only while it stays visible. The extension also verifies the study pages you choose and pauses scoring on other tabs. Normal blinking is expected. Looking down far enough that the camera loses your eyes creates a visible data gap instead of lowering your score.

      Use the result to study changes in task focus, then compare it with what you completed and how the work felt. It is an estimate rather than a diagnosis, and it does not read pupil dilation, assess health, or predict grades. Frames stay in this browser and are not saved or uploaded.

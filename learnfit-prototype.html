A NOTE FROM THE MAKER / READ ME FIRST

Hi. This is a project called LearnFit.

I made it because so much advice about studying is weirdly loud: study for this many minutes, rest for that many minutes, repeat until you feel guilty. I wanted to make something quieter. LearnFit starts with your baseline and helps you notice changes as you work, so you can experiment, adjust, and find a way of studying that actually fits your life.

It is still an experiment. The number on screen is not your worth, your attention span, or a diagnosis. It is just one small signal you can use alongside how you feel.

— Xiyue, building in public
THINGS I CHANGED WHILE MAKING THIS

build log

v0.1
early

I started with a score. It felt wrong.

The first version tried to label “focus.” That sounded confident, but it wasn’t honest about what a webcam can know.

v0.4
later

I replaced the score with a personal baseline.

Now the app compares small changes with the same person’s own session, and explains the ingredients.

v0.6

I added pauses, reflections, and a real exit ramp.

You can stop, write what helped, and choose what to try next. No streaks. No leaderboard.
